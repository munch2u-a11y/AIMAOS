# RESEARCH BRIEF: Regional Logistics & Warehouse Efficiency Benchmarks

**Prepared by**: Research Specialist (`Quinn`)  
**Target Client**: Acme Logistics Solutions  
**Date**: 2026-07-28  

---

## 1. Executive Findings
Analysis of regional 3PL warehouse operations in Q2 2026 demonstrates an average facility dock-to-stock cycle time of 3.4 hours. Acme's current baseline of 5.8 hours represents a 70% latency penalty compared to industry benchmarks.

## 2. Key Bottleneck Factors
1. **Manual Receiving Verification**: Lack of automated barcode scan verification at receiving docks adds ~1.2 hours per shipment.
2. **Suboptimal Pallet Routing**: Unoptimized fork-truck routing in Aisles B and C accounts for 40% of internal transit delays.
3. **Inventory Discrepancy Reconciliation**: Periodic manual stock counts stall order fulfillment during peak hours.

## 3. Strategic Recommendations
- Implement automated barcode scanning protocols at intake receiving bays.
- Re-route high-velocity SKUs to forward fulfillment zones near primary loading bays.
- Transition from manual nightly counts to cycle-counting automation.
